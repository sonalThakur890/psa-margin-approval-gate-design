Margin guidance and gate

Prototype for epic FUL-11893. Same screens as PSA_Margin_Screens_V2_doc (25 Nov 2026). Landscaping first. Every example is a real PAM agreement; where a value was changed to show a behaviour, the screen says so.

The rule

Property margin = customer $ minus provider $, divided by customer $. It counts every PSA on the store for that term sheet, each line × its visits, over the same period.

Property margin (default limits, set per trade)Route
25% or moreNo approval: straight to Awaiting Signature
0% to under 25%Level 1: Regional Manager
Below 0%Level 1: Regional Manager, then Level 2: Leadership

Year-on-year drop: if the property margin is 10 points or more below last season (limit per trade), the PSA goes to Level 1, then Level 2, even at 25% or more. The stricter route applies.

Each PSA routes on its own property, so a losing property does not hold up healthy ones. RFP review is optional, from Level 2 only, by hand: V2. On Draft, more provider visits than the customer contracted is flagged and needs a reason; equal or fewer is not flagged.

How the margin is calculated

worked for row 3, 7-Eleven (38750)
What one margin covers: one property, for this term sheet’s service line (Landscaping), adding every service type and every PSA on that property, over the period in the orange header. It is not per service type and not per PSA.
Customer $ = the total the customer is charged for this property in the period, adding up every service type on the term sheet.
Provider $ = the total we pay providers for this property in the period, adding up every PSA on the property: each service’s provider price times its visits.
Margin = customer $ minus provider $, divided by customer $.
Worked for row 3, 7-Eleven (38750): term sheet R26-00000QZQ has 4 service types — Routine Landscaping Service (customer pays monthly) and Mulching, Tree Pruning, Palm Tree Pruning (tracking only). In 1 Nov – 31 Dec only Routine Landscaping Service is scheduled, and the property has 1 PSA (PSA-261106-00077). So:
Customer $ = $671.39 a month times 2 months = $1,342.78
Provider $ = $160.00 a visit times 5 visits (3 in Nov, 2 in Dec) = $800.00
Margin = $1,342.78 minus $800.00, divided by $1,342.78 = 40.4%
Monthly customer charges and per-visit provider prices are totalled for the same period before comparing, which is why there is no margin under each price. A line with no provider price yet is left out and listed. Press Recalculate after changing a price.
